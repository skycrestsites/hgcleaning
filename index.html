What areas do you serve?

We serve Salt Lake City, Utah and the surrounding areas, including Sandy, Draper, Murray, Holladay, Millcreek, South Jordan, West Jordan and more. Not sure if we cover your area? Just ask.

Are your cleaners background-checked?

Yes. Every member of our team is background-checked before they ever step into a client's home or business.

Yes. We arrive with professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you prefer specific products, we are happy to use them.

How do I get a price?

Book online for instant pricing or give us a call. We will ask a few questions about your space and the service you need, then provide clear, upfront pricing.

We do. Residential cleaning is our focus, and we also provide commercial cleaning for offices, retail spaces and commercial properties.
