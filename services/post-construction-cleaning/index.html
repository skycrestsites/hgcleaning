Overview

What to expect from your post-construction cleaning

Renovations and new builds leave behind a layer of fine dust that settles on every surface, inside cabinets and in vents. Standard cleaning simply is not built for it. Our post-construction service is.

We work top to bottom, removing dust, debris, paint specks and adhesive residue so the finished space looks the way it was meant to. We coordinate with homeowners, contractors and builders to fit your timeline.

FAQ

Post-Construction Cleaning questions

When should post-construction cleaning be scheduled?

Once major work, painting and installations are complete. If trades are still finishing small items, we can schedule a rough clean and a final clean.

Do you remove large construction debris?

We remove dust, light debris and residue. Large debris such as lumber, drywall scraps and dumpster loads should be cleared by the contractor first.

Do you work with contractors directly?

Yes. We are happy to coordinate timing and access with your builder or general contractor.
