Overview

What to expect from your deep cleaning

Over time, dust, soap scum and grease settle into the corners of every home. Our deep cleaning service is built to remove that buildup. We slow down, work room by room and give attention to the details that make a home feel genuinely clean.

Deep cleaning is the best starting point for new clients, seasonal refreshes and homes preparing for guests or special occasions. Once your home is reset, a recurring standard clean keeps it that way.

FAQ

Deep Cleaning questions

How often should I book a deep clean?

Most homes benefit from a deep clean every three to six months, with standard cleanings in between to maintain the results.

How long does a deep clean take?

A deep clean typically takes longer than a standard visit, often four to eight hours depending on the size and condition of the home. Larger homes may have a bigger team assigned.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Our team arrives with professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you prefer a specific product in your home, just let us know.
