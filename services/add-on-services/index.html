Overview

Go beyond the standard checklist

Every home is different, and so is every to-do list. Our add-on services let you go beyond the standard checklist and take care of the tasks that are easy to put off.

Add-ons can be included with a standard, deep, recurring, move-in or move-out cleaning. Simply select the extras you want when you book online and we will build them into your visit.

How add-ons work

Simple, flexible and priced upfront.

  • Select any add-ons when you book online
  • Pair them with any residential cleaning service
  • Each add-on is priced clearly before you confirm
  • Completed during the same visit as your clean

Add-On Services questions

Can I book an add-on by itself?

Add-ons are designed to be paired with one of our cleaning services. Reach out and we will recommend the best fit for what you need.

Each add-on is priced individually based on size and scope. You will see the price of each add-on when you book, so there are no surprises.
