Overview

What to expect from your move-in / move-out cleaning

Moving is stressful enough. Our move-in and move-out service takes cleaning off your list. With the home empty, we can reach every surface, including the inside of cabinets, closets and appliances that are usually hidden.

We work with renters hoping to get their deposit back, homeowners preparing to list or move in, and landlords and property managers turning over units between tenants.

FAQ

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning questions

Does the home need to be empty?

For the best results, yes. An empty home lets us reach inside cabinets, closets and behind where furniture used to be.

Do you clean inside appliances?

Yes. Move-in and move-out cleanings include the inside of the refrigerator, oven and microwave.

Can you work with my property manager?

Absolutely. We regularly coordinate access and timing with landlords and property managers.
